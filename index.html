THE TRUTH I NEED YOU TO KNOW

I have been taking you for granted.

I kept acting like my work could wait for nothing—except you. I let my schedule, stress, and tiredness become excuses. I kept saying “later,” believing there would always be more time, while making you feel like you had to compete for mine.

You deserved to feel chosen, heard, and important in my life. Instead, I made you feel alone beside me. I understand that good intentions do not erase the hurt I caused, and I am not going to make excuses for it anymore.

WHAT I WANT TO CHANGE

I do not want to offer another promise without showing you the difference.

I want to make real time for us, not leave you with whatever is left after everything else. I want to be fully present, listen without defending myself, and choose you in the quiet, everyday ways that make love feel safe.

FROM ME, WITH NO EXCUSES

I AM SO,
SO SORRY.

I am sorry for the times I made you feel second to my work. I am sorry for every moment you needed me and I was only half there. I am sorry for not making your importance in my life clear, constant, and loving.

You have always mattered to me. I failed to make you feel that—and I understand why that has brought us here.

I cannot ask you to forget the hurt or rush you to forgive me. I only hope you will let me prove, patiently and honestly, that I can love you better than I have.

With all my love,
Yours
Made for the person who deserves to feel chosen.
